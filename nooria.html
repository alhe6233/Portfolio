Nooria — Community Q&A App for iOS

Wissen. Teilen. Gemeinsam wachsen. Nooria is a mobile app where people ask questions anonymously, get honest answers from the community and grow through a rank system.

📱 iOS App 💬 Community Q&A 🏅 Rank System 🔥 Firebase
About the App

What is Nooria?

Nooria brings people together around shared knowledge. Users can search existing questions, ask their own — optionally anonymously — and answer others. Every contribution earns points, so helping the community is rewarded with higher ranks.

❓

Ask & Answer

Post questions, search through existing ones and get answers from the community.

🕶️

Anonymous & Honest

Questions can be asked anonymously, so people feel free to ask what really matters to them.

🏆

Ranks & Progress

Points for every contribution unlock ranks from Bronze up to Crown.

🎥
