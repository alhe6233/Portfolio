AI-Powered Personalized Skincare Consultation Platform

SelfGlow is an AI-powered skincare e-commerce platform that helps users understand their skin and find suitable skincare products through personalized digital consultation.

Project Presentation

About the Project

SelfGlow combines a traditional skincare e-commerce platform with AI-powered consultation features. The goal is to make skincare shopping more personal and accessible by helping users understand their skin, identify suitable products, and receive recommendations based on their individual needs.

The platform includes a product catalogue with filters, an AI-powered RAG chatbot, AI-based photo skin analysis, a skin quiz, personalized product recommendations, weather-based skincare tips, and a creative AI-generated “Glow” preview.
